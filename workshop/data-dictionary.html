707 rows · one row per award · 2024-02 to 2026-06

Survival bias. Only people who stayed long enough receive a 3-Year Service Award. Comparing their attrition rate to non-recipients doesn't measure the award's effect — it measures who stayed long enough to get one. The award didn't reduce attrition; attrition selected who got the award.
