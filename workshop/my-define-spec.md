## Question Spec

# Step 1: Restate and set the population

**Restated question:** Did engagement meaningfully improve?
**Population:** All regular employees who responded to the survey

# Step 2: Time basis and grain

**Time basis:** since 2023 year over year
**Grain:** one row per response per item

# Step 3: Name the measure

**Measure:** Engagement is an average of the 3 enagement items, ENG01, ENG02, and ENG03. "Improved" is a 3 point gain year over year after converting to a percentage. We will use Top Box scoring where for the 5 point Likert Scale, 4s and 5s are "agree" and for the 7 point Likert Scale, 5, 6, and 7 are "agree". 

# Step 4: Comparison, decision, precision

**Comparison:** against the 2023 baseline
**Decision it informs:** if engagement is flat, we'll look at driver analysis to determine what levers we can pull to increase engagement 
**Precision needed:** up at least 3 points

# Step 5: Existing coverage and open assumptions

**Existing coverage:** none, this is the exercise
**Open assumptions:** that a 3 point change is actually a meaninful difference, That "engagement" means the three ENG items and not all six, and That the Top Box cutoffs you defined (4–5 on a 5-point scale, 5–7 on a 7-point scale) are the ones your organization actually uses
